WHY DIFFERENT PATHS?
  TCP:  8 → 5 → 1 → 0 (started at t=6s, found shortest path)
  UDP:  8 → 5 → 2 → 1 → 0 (started at t=7s, different route discovered)
  
  This is NORMAL HWMP behavior:
    • Reactive routing protocol
    • On-demand route discovery
    • Link quality changes over time
    • Different flows can take different optimal paths

WHY UDP HAS HIGHER DELAY?
  1. Extra hop: 4 vs 3 (+33%)
  2. Queue delays in mesh forwarding
  3. No congestion control (constant sending rate)
  4. Channel contention (CSMA/CA backoff)
