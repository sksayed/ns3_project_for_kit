SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
Minimum:
- OS: Linux (Ubuntu 20.04+), WSL2
- RAM: 8 GB
- Disk: 5 GB free
- CPU: 2 cores
- Compiler: g++-11 or clang++-17

Recommended:
- RAM: 16 GB
- CPU: 4+ cores
- Compiler: g++-12 or g++-13
